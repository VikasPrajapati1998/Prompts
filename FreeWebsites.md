# Free Websites & Web Apps

A collection of free, privacy-friendly websites and web applications. No account required unless noted.

## Video Editing

### [OpenCut](https://opencut.app)
The best browser-based alternative to CapCut.

- **Type**: Web app (runs entirely in the browser)
- **Account**: Not required
- **Installation**: None - just open and start editing
- **Platform**: Works on any platform
- **Privacy**: Open source and built for privacy

## AI & Productivity

### [AnythingLLM](https://anythingllm.com)
A self-hostable AI platform and desktop app.

- **Type**: Web app / self-hostable platform / desktop app
- **Account**: Not required
- **Features**: Chat with your documents and run AI using large language models entirely on your own machine
- **Privacy**: Runs locally - your data stays with you

### [Handy](https://handy.computer)
A free alternative to WhisperFlow (desktop app).

- **Type**: Desktop speech-to-text tool
- **Account**: Not required
- **How it works**: Press a keyboard shortcut, speak, release - your words appear wherever your cursor is
- **Works with**: Any text field on your computer
- **Privacy**: No cloud, no subscription, no copy-paste step

## Music

### [Nuclear](https://nuclearplayer.com)
An open-source desktop music player that streams audio from multiple online sources.

- **Type**: Desktop app
- **Account**: Not required
- **Features**: Stream audio from multiple online sources with no ads and no subscription
- **Privacy**: No tracking, no telemetry, no data collection. Source code is publicly auditable

## Screen Recording

### [Cap](https://cap.so)
Record and edit your screen recordings in seconds.

- **Type**: Cross-platform screen recorder
- **Account**: Not required
- **Platform**: Native apps for macOS, Windows, and Linux
- **Features**: Three distinct workflows in one app
- **Built for**: People who share work asynchronously

# Open-Source & Self-Hosted Alternatives to Popular SaaS Applications

A curated list of open-source and self-hosted applications that can replace popular commercial SaaS platforms. These solutions provide greater control over data, privacy, customization, and infrastructure.

| # | Application | Alternative to | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[Immich](https://immich.app/)** | Google Photos | Self-hosted photo and video backup, automatic synchronization, photo organization, and facial recognition. |
| 2 | **[OpenProject](https://www.openproject.org/)** | Asana | Project management, task tracking, Gantt charts, Agile boards, and team collaboration. |
| 3 | **[Cal.com](https://cal.com/)** | Calendly | Online appointment scheduling, booking links, calendar synchronization, and meeting management. |
| 4 | **[OpenWhispr](https://openwhispr.com/)** | Whisper Flow | AI-powered voice typing, speech-to-text transcription, and offline voice input. |

| 8 | **[Chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox)** | ElevenLabs | AI-powered text-to-speech, voice cloning, multilingual speech generation, and Hindi voice support. |

## Key Objectives

- **Data Ownership:** Host applications and manage data on your own infrastructure.
- **Privacy & Security:** Maintain greater control over sensitive information and user data.
- **Cost Optimization:** Reduce recurring subscription costs associated with commercial SaaS platforms.
- **Customization:** Modify and extend applications according to specific business requirements.
- **AI Integration:** Leverage local AI models and self-hosted AI capabilities wherever supported.
- **Vendor Independence:** Reduce dependency on third-party platforms and proprietary ecosystems.

## Deployment Approach

Where supported, prioritize self-hosted deployment using Docker and Docker Compose. Use local storage, databases, and AI models where appropriate to maintain control over infrastructure and operational costs.
